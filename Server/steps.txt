Step 4: text ko chunks mein todna. Ye RAG ka sabse important concept hai, 
Chunking kyun?

PDF ka pura text ek saath embed karoge to dikkat hogi:

Embedding model ki input limit hoti hai.
Pure document ka ek vector bana to wo “average” meaning ban jaata hai, 
aur kisi specific sawaal ka answer dhoondhna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Isliye text ko chhote tukdon (chunks) mein todte hain, aur har chunk ka apna vector hota hai.
 Sawaal pucha to sirf wahi 3-4 chunks milte hain jo relevant hain.
Overlap kyun? Agar koi important sentence do chunks ke beech kat gaya, 
to dono mein meaning adhuri reh jaati hai. Isliye har chunk apne pichle
 chunk ke last ~200 characters repeat karta hai.

Embedding model text leta hai aur ek lambi list of numbers deta hai, jaise [0.012, -0.834, 0.201, ...]. 
Ye numbers text ke meaning ko represent karte hain. Do chunks ka meaning similar ho to unke vectors 
paas-paas hote hain. Sawaal bhi embed karenge, aur jo chunks sawaal 
ke vector ke sabse paas honge wahi answer dene ke kaam aayenge. Ye search Step 7 mein karenge.
